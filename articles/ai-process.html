How AI Is Changing Business Process Engineering

A practical management perspective on an important information technology concept.

Artificial intelligence is changing business process engineering by moving organizations beyond basic automation. Traditional process improvement focuses on removing waste, reducing delays, and standardizing work. AI adds another layer: systems can classify information, identify patterns, generate recommendations, and support decisions.

From automation to augmentation

Automation is useful when a task follows clear rules. AI becomes more valuable when the work includes large amounts of information, repeated judgment, forecasting, or pattern recognition.

Why data quality matters

AI depends on reliable data. If business data is incomplete, duplicated, outdated, or inconsistent, the resulting output can also be unreliable.

Management implication

Managers should treat AI as part of process design rather than as a separate technology project. Strong implementations connect technology, people, controls, and measurable business outcomes.
