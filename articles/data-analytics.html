DATA ANALYTICS

Why Data Analytics Improves Business Decisions

A practical management perspective on an important information technology concept.

Organizations generate large amounts of operational and customer data, but data has little value unless it is converted into useful information. Data analytics helps managers identify patterns, compare performance, understand causes, and evaluate alternatives.

Descriptive to predictive thinking

Descriptive analytics explains what happened. Diagnostic analytics explores why it happened. Predictive analytics estimates what may happen next, while prescriptive analytics helps recommend possible actions.

Dashboards and KPIs

Dashboards can make complex information easier to understand, but they should focus on measures connected to business objectives. Too many metrics can create noise instead of insight.

Management implication

Analytics supports better judgment, but it does not eliminate the need for context. Good decisions combine reliable data, business knowledge, stakeholder understanding, and awareness of uncertainty.
